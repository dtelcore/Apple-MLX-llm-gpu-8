### Prepare script

New [`tools/prepare_tinystories.py`](tools/prepare_tinystories.py). Source is `roneneldan/TinyStories` only (train parquet shards plus the validation shard). No `data/train.txt`.

Output under gitignored `data/tinystories/`:

- `text/train-XXXXX.txt` and `text/valid.txt`: one story per line, internal newlines collapsed.
- `vocab.json`: BPE trained only on TinyStories, `bpe_merges=6000` (4–8k vocab). Pass a `max_chars` of tens of millions into `BPETokenizer.from_corpus`. The 200k default in [`tokenizer/bpe.py`](tokenizer/bpe.py) is a sample cap and must be overridden here.
- `tokens/train-XXXXX.npy` and `tokens/valid.npy` as int32, plus `manifest.json` (shard order, vocab sha, token counts).

Why a new loader: [`setup/config_loader.py`](setup/config_loader.py) loads one text file into a `list[str]`, and [`training/dataset.py`](training/dataset.py) keeps the stream as int64. Full TinyStories does not fit beside the model on this Air that way.

### Loader hook

If the recipe sets `dataset.vocab_path` and `dataset.token_dir`, [`train.py`](train.py) `build_tokenizer_and_config` loads that vocab and the unguided session builds `WindowedDataset` from a concatenated int32 memmap. Cabinet recipes omit both fields and keep today’s path. No memory-controller edit. The new recipe has no `dataset.path`, so a full text reload cannot start.

### Config, policy, probe

Copy the shape of [`setup/story_c256_l6_config.json`](setup/story_c256_l6_config.json).

- [`setup/english_tinystories_c256_l6_config.json`](setup/english_tinystories_c256_l6_config.json): C=256, L=6, H=8, T=256, `residual_scale: true`, `combine: false`, `layer_strategy: resident`, lr `9e-4`, warmup 1000, `min_lr_ratio` 0.1, B=4, accum=4 (4096 tokens/step). `dataset.vocab_path` and `dataset.token_dir` point at `data/tinystories/`.
- [`setup/unguided_tinystories_policy.json`](setup/unguided_tinystories_policy.json): fresh dir `output/checkpoints/english_tinystories_c256_l6`, `max_steps` 50000 (~200M tokens; raise later toward 1B), `eval_every` 500, early-stop patience too large for a calm val loss to stop the run, `max_wall_s` 259200 (72h), `probe_mode: tinystories`, `probe_every: 2000`.

Probe hook in [`training/unguided/prober.py`](training/unguided/prober.py), [`training/unguided/eval_suite.py`](training/unguided/eval_suite.py), and the stop loop in [`unguided_trainer.py`](unguided_trainer.py). Cabinet probe path stays untouched. `tinystories` is val-only (same as `english`: no cabinet remix) and generates this fixed set at temperature 0.8 for 160 new tokens:

- `Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lily who found a`
- `Tell me a short story about a brave mouse.`
- `Who are you?`
- one paraphrase of a cabinet fact (must not dump the stored Assistant line)
- `What is the capital of Atlantis?`
- `What is 17 + 4?` (miss check only; arithmetic stays in the router)

Success is coherence in `generate_probe.md`. 10k steps is not a stopping point.

Unit test: config/policy parse, the fixed prompt list, and the prepare script’s line-collapse plus manifest writer on a fake shard. No network download in tests.
